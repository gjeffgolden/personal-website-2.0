Welcome to the new blog. I built this section to have a low-friction place to
write — about engineering, leadership, the outdoors, and whatever else is on my
mind.

## Why write?

Writing forces clarity. As [Barry Lopez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barry_Lopez)
put it, *"everything is held together with stories."* I want a space to think
through ideas in public rather than letting them evaporate.

## What to expect

A few themes I'll keep coming back to:

- **Engineering** — practical notes from building and shipping software.
- **Leadership** — what I'm learning about managing teams well.
- **The trail** — the occasional dispatch from the mountains.
